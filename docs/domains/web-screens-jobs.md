# Domain: Web Screens & Jobs

**One line:** The FastAPI/HTMX screens (Users, Groups, Calendars, plus Lifecycle/Signatures) that render read-only pages and drive GAM mutations, and the in-memory job registry (`web/jobs.py`, one bounded `Job` base) that lets long per-user loops report polled progress instead of hanging the request — listed on every page by the header's jobs tray, with Stop (plan U5).

**Owns invariant(s):** #9 (bound anything polled) — every live progress feed keeps a fixed rolling window and caps retained lists so a domain-wide run doesn't grow each 1s poll with the user count.
**Enforcement home:** `tests/test_write_routes_guarded.py` — every POST route refuses a bare POST with zero `gam` writes unless exempt with a reason, the confirm step each preview renders writes nothing when posted without its confirmation (token and all) and really runs the write when posted whole, a confirm step that posts the page's form runs nothing once that form is edited after the preview (`Case.edit`), and a replayed one writes nothing more; `tests/test_previews.py` pins the store itself. `tests/test_users_web.py` — the scale guards live there: `test_every_job_record_stays_bounded_at_scale` (the base's bounds, for `Job`, `BatchJob` and `OnboardJob` alike), `test_run_subscribe_bounds_its_feed_at_scale`, `test_signatures_apply_final_summary_caps_failed_list`, `test_batch_job_failures_stay_bounded_and_render_the_overflow`, `test_run_subscribe_caps_its_failed_sample`, `test_each_bulk_feed_says_why_a_target_failed`, and the source tripwire `test_no_loop_appends_to_a_jobs_failed_list_directly` (it scans all of `gamgui/`, since some loops now live in core) (plus the cross-tenant `test_calendars_search_ignores_other_domains_index`). `tests/test_jobs_tray.py` — Stop on each loop through the strict mock (the exact argv that ran), the Stop route's refusals, the tray's bound and the job page. `tests/test_retry_failures.py` — retry failures only, through the strict mock's `*missing*` trigger: the preview writes nothing, its Apply needs the token and the confirm step and writes only the failed people, and past the failure cap there is no retry. `tests/test_calendar_index.py` covers the persistent index itself (CRUD, atomic swap, owner-only file, scan), not the #9 guards. `tests/test_a11y.py` (`make a11y`, CI's `a11y` job) is the accessibility ratchet over every screen and a keyboard-only walk (below), and in the default run reads every template for an unnamed form field, a `[data-focus]` that can't take focus, a `focus:outline-none` with no `focus:ring-`, and a tab strip that isn't an ARIA tablist; `tests/test_contrast.py`, in the default run, holds every text colour in `app.css` to 4.5:1 on paper and white, every fill under a white label, and every text field's, select's and textarea's border to 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11: `border-brand-field`, the gray darkened — brand-gray at 40–60% was ~1.5:1; card and rule borders are decoration and stay light). No drift-guard/hook — plain pytest.

## Files
- `gamgui/web/jobs.py` — the one bounded job base, `Job`: `record(item, ok, reason, detail)` counts one target (`done`, `applied`, `failed_total`), keeps a failure as an `Outcome` in the `failed` sample capped at `FAILED_SAMPLE_CAP` (200; `more` is the "+K more" past it), cuts its `reason` (the remediation in words) and `detail` (GAM's raw error) to `DETAIL_CAP` (300 chars), and rolls the live feed `recent` to the newest `window` rows (`RECENT_WINDOW`, 12); `finish()` sets `finished`/`finished_at`; `cancel_requested` is Stop's flag (plan U5, below); `interrupted` is offboarding's cut-off flag; `title` is what the tray calls a job and `kind` a `PANELS` key — the status route that renders its panel (`status_url`), whether it can stop (`can_stop`: every kind but `index`, one domain-wide read) and whether Stop asks first (`confirm_stop`: `offboard`); `retry` is what "Retry the N that failed" re-runs (a signature's template, a department) and `can_retry` says whether it may (below); `retry_of(jobs, id, kind)` is the finished job a retry previews, or why not, and `retry_key(id)` the preview-store key it holds its people under. `BatchJob(Job)` adds offboarding's per-step `log` and `skipped`; `start_job(jobs, total, keep=10, window=12, title=, kind=)` registers one; `register_job` registers any job, pruning the oldest finished; `tray(jobs)` is the header's list — at most `TRAY_ROWS` (8), running first, then newest first — the running count and how many it leaves out. Signatures run a plain `Job`, onboarding its `OnboardJob(Job)`. The shared polled-progress record on `AppState.jobs`.
- `gamgui/core/bulk.py` — `stop_reason(kind, remediation, left)`: the message a per-user loop stops with when a write fails with an account-wide kind (`ACCOUNT_WIDE_KINDS`: sign-in expired, GAM not set up, a scope not granted), else None; `stop_requested(job)`: true, with `job.error` = "Stopped by you — N not attempted.", once Stop set `cancel_requested` (every loop asks it before each target); and `set_departments(job, conn, users, department)`, the bulk department loop (each person's title kept). Both moved out of `web/` (plan Q9); a loop in core fills the job it is handed and never imports `web/`.
- `gamgui/core/clock.py` — `now()`, a monotonic clock that counts time asleep (macOS `time.monotonic()` doesn't). Every TTL uses it: preview expiry, the directory cache, the Keychain secret cache, the onboarding credentials sheet.
- `gamgui/web/previews.py` — `Previews` on `AppState.previews`: what a confirm step may run. A preview route `hold(flow, form_key, value)`s the values it showed and renders the token into its confirm control (`preview` in `hx-vals` or a hidden input); the apply route `take(flow, token, form_key)`s them — single use, refused when expired (`PREVIEW_TTL`, 15 min) or when the live form no longer matches the previewed one — and runs the held values, never ones rebuilt from the live form. At most `PREVIEWS_KEPT` (8) per flow (#9). A held preview is also bound to the tenant it was made on (`Previews.bind`, wired to the active connector's domain in `AppState.__post_init__`): taken under another domain it is refused ("The active domain changed after the preview"), and a tenant switch (`AppState.activate`) clears the store outright — a confirm step left open across a switch once ran its old-tenant values, or a bare name GAM completes with the new domain, through the new tenant's credentials (review 2, R5; `tests/test_tenant_switch.py`).
- `gamgui/web/routes/users.py` — list/search/detail + actions. The list (plan U8): the search matches name, address, title, department and org unit; any column sorts both ways (`SORTS`, ties by name, a blank title last) over the whole cached list before the page is cut; `PAGE_SIZES` 15/25/50 (15 fit the 1100×760 window with the filters on the title row and 30 px rows; a longer page scrolls inside the table, whose header sticks and whose pager stays on screen). The view is the URL: `_list_state` checks each value (an unknown scope, column or size is the default), `_list_url` writes only what differs from the default, `/users` renders any view it is given, and `/users/table` answers `HX-Replace-Url` with it — replaced, not pushed, so a search typed a pause at a time doesn't leave a Back step per pause. Each row's detail link carries the view as `back`, and the detail page's "← Users" is `_list_url_from(back)`: only the list's own keys, re-checked, so it can only reopen `/users` — the native window has no Back button, so that link is its Back. A sort header is a button with a stable id (htmx gives focus back to it), `aria-sort` on its `th`; a Prev/Next or Clear filters click (`HX-Trigger` in `_PAGER_IDS`) puts focus on the count line. Actions: (signature, delegate, groups, calendar ACLs, org/role, vacation, suspend via guard, delete, bulk set-department job — `_run_bulk_store` is the job's task: `bulk.set_departments`, each accepted write patching that person's cached record through `on_set`, and any failure dropping the whole list at the end; it, `bulk_store.html` and the `store` form field keep their old internal names). A failed action says what didn't happen and GAM's remediation in words, with the raw error in `_action_result.html`'s collapsed "GAM's error" (`write_failed`; delegation does the same through its `notice`).
- `gamgui/web/routes/calendars.py` — find calendar, ACL detail, event search/delete, secondary-calendar delete, share+subscribe fan-out job, persistent index rebuild job. `/share` resolves who it would subscribe *before* writing: a group of `guard.DEFAULT_BULK_THRESHOLD` (10) or more gets a confirm step in `_calendar_detail.html` naming the count (and the first ten members), holding the resolved members under a single-use token (flow `calendar_share_group`); its Confirm posts the live share form (`hx-include="#cal-share-form"`, which keeps the typed target and role) to `/share/group`, which takes the token, refuses a changed calendar/group/role, runs `guard.enforce(..., confirm_step=True)` and only then grants the ACL and starts the fan-out. It once started the fan-out straight from the Share button, whatever the group's size (a GATED case in `tests/test_write_routes_guarded.py`; the mock's `allhands@example.com` has 12 members). A failed `/unshare`, `/delete` or `/event/delete` answers with `write_failed` like the user-detail writes (they once headlined GAM's raw line).
- `gamgui/web/routes/jobs.py` — the jobs tray (plan U5). `GET /jobs/status` is the tray's list (`_jobs_tray.html`), polled every 2s from `base.html` on every page, with the header's "N running" badge out of band (`_job_live.html` `jobs_count`); `GET /jobs/<id>` (`job.html`) is a job's own page, whose panel loads from its `status_url` — the route that started it renders it, so nothing is duplicated (the index job's container is `#cal-index`, the id its panel targets); `POST /jobs/stop` sets `cancel_requested` and answers `_job_stop.html` in the button's place. It makes no GAM call (`LOCAL_ONLY` in the tripwire). `server.py` registers `jobs_tray(request)` as a Jinja global for `base.html`, and `active_tenant(request)` for its tenant chip (`_tenant.html`, plan U10b — see the setup runbook).
- `gamgui/web/routes/_common.py` — the helpers every screen shares, one copy each: `app_state`, `connector`, `friendly(exc, fallback)` (GAM's remediation, else the screen's own fallback line), `error_partial` (the amber `_action_result.html`), `write_failed`, `signature_store`, `NOT_CONNECTED`. They were pasted into each route module (seven `_friendly`s, five `_err`s) and had begun to drift; `test_route_helpers_live_once_in_common` fails on a new local copy.
- `gamgui/web/routes/groups.py` — the Groups board (`/groups`, plans U7 and A6). A group finder (`/search`) and the add field's type-ahead (`/people`) match address or name over the cached `st.groups()` / `st.users()` and render at most `PICK_LIMIT` (15), with "keep typing" past it — nothing renders the whole directory (the old board drew every user as a drag card and ran a live `gam print groups` on each visit). `/members` is a picked group's panel (`_group_panel.html`: heading, the add form, a member filter) and `/members/list` one page of `MEMBERS_PAGE` (50) members (`_board_members.html`: owners, then managers, then members; a role column; a nested group links back to the board; `/groups?group=` opens one directly, as a user's group chips do). Add (`POST /members`, a single-target LOW write) takes a role that `GAMCommands.add_group_member` validates — a `ValueError` becomes "Pick a role" with no `gam` call — and one full address: the field is free text and GAM reads the member as a `<UserTypeEntity>`, so `oauthuser` would add the authorizing admin, a bare name `name@<domain>` and a comma two people; add and remove refuse a group or member that fails `looks_like_email`, in words, before GAM. A member with no address (the domain-wide CUSTOMER row) has no Remove…. Remove is a confirm step: a row's Remove… posts `/members/remove/preview` (`_group_remove_confirm.html`, which warns before removing an owner) and its Remove posts `/members/remove`, which runs `guard.enforce(..., confirm_step=True)` first. A user's Groups tab (`users.py` `/groups`, `_groups.html`) removes the same way: a chip's × posts `/users/groups/remove/preview`, which renders that confirm step into `#user-group-confirm` posting back to `/users/groups/remove` (GATED in the tripwire; it once removed on the × alone), and its add picker lists the cached `st.groups()`, not a live `print groups` per open. A failed write re-renders the list with `_action_result.html` above it: what didn't happen and the remediation, GAM's error collapsed (plan U9). There is no drag-and-drop any more: every action is a form control. `groups.js` holds the finder's `aria-current` and the add field's ARIA combobox (below).
- `gamgui/web/templates/_bulk_apply.html`, `_calendar_subscribe_job.html`, `_calendar_index_job.html`, `_sequence_run.html` — the self-polling HTMX partials; `_job_failures.html`'s `failures(job)` macro is the final panels' failed list (item — reason, GAM's error in a `<details>`, "… +K more"). `_job_live.html` holds `stop(job, where)` — the Stop button in each running panel and tray row, "Stopping after the current one…" once pressed — and `ended(job, where)`, the same id on the finished panel's line (below).

## How it works
Routes read from the cached directory (`request.app.state.gamgui.users()`) so pages/searches are instant. A write whose new values are known patches its one record (`st.patch_user`, plan U12: a title/department save, suspend/unsuspend, a delete — no fields means the record goes — and each accepted write of a bulk department run) instead of dropping the list, so the next page doesn't re-run `gam print users`; `UserCache.patch` builds a new list (a caller iterating the old one isn't changed under it), an address that isn't a cached primary drops the whole list, and the list keeps the age of its last full fetch, so the TTL and `force` (Users → Refresh) still re-read everything. A fetch that raced a patch or `invalidate` is returned but not kept — including one that landed while the call still waited on the cache lock: `get` reads the generation on entry, when its caller had already bound `fetch` to a connector, so a read queued across a tenant switch can't store the old tenant's list as the new one's (review 2, R9). **Stale-while-refreshing (plan U12b):** a page that shows the list's age passes `stale_ok=True` — the Users table, Reports, the Groups finder and its people type-ahead, onboarding's group picker and the Builder's `/pick` — and past the TTL gets the old list at once while `UserCache` runs one background refresh (`_refresh_soon`: one task at a time, kept only if no patch or `invalidate` — a tenant switch — landed since it was asked for; a failed one is logged and the old list stays). Each of those pages renders `_as_of.html` ("as of 3 min ago, refreshing"; `routes/_common.as_of` passes the cache only when something is cached; the `ago` filter is in `server.py`) with a Refresh that forces a re-read (`refresh=1`) — except the two dropdown type-aheads, whose label is text only. User detail is `stale_ok` too, with the same label: its Refresh (`refresh=1`) is a direct `info user` read of that one account, never a full `print users`, as is a user not in the cached list. Every other caller leaves `stale_ok` off and reads fresh or raises: offboarding's `_check` must never decide on a stale list; `guard.alias_deletes`' lookups never read the cache at all (live `info user`). `tests/test_cache_freshness.py` holds both. Offboarding drops the list when its run starts and again when it finishes (`_run_offboard`: a page loaded mid-run re-read the leaver as they were), and a tenant switch drops it (`AppState.activate`, which on a new domain also clears held previews and the Builder's last result, review 2 R11) — what they change isn't a known set of fields; mutations are HTMX POSTs that swap a small result region. A long per-user loop (`_run_bulk_store`, `_run_subscribe`, `_build_index`, offboarding's `lifecycle.run_offboard`) is fired with `asyncio.create_task`, its handle stashed on `job.task` (strong ref so it isn't GC'd), and the route returns a partial that self-polls a `.../status?job=<id>` endpoint on `hx-trigger="load delay, every 1s"` until `job.finished`, then renders a final panel with no more polling. `start_job` prunes the oldest finished jobs (keep=10) so the registry can't grow forever; `_lifespan` cancels any in-flight `job.task` on shutdown; the write it was running has its `gam` killed and is audited as interrupted (`_run_write`), not silently dropped. The registry is also the header's **jobs tray** (plan U5): a job survives navigating away — its row keeps its progress and final state, and its title links to `/jobs/<id>`, its panel — but not a quit (jobs live in memory).

## Invariants & the failure history
- **A per-user loop stops at a failure every later user shares.** `_run_apply` (signatures), `bulk.set_departments`, `_run_subscribe` and `_run_bulk_onboard` (on an account-create failure) read `ChangeResult.kind` after each write and, on an `ACCOUNT_WIDE_KINDS` kind, set `job.error` to `stop_reason(...)` ("Stopped: <remediation> The remaining N were not attempted.") and break; the final panel says so with how many were done. A per-target failure (not found, one refusal) keeps going. The signature loop once ran on through the whole company after the admin's sign-in expired, one identical failure per mailbox (failure-log 2026-09-23; `tests/test_bulk_stop.py`). Not these, on purpose: the Builder sequence (heterogeneous steps; a Gmail scope missing says nothing about the next step) and offboarding (its steps stop through `requires`).
- **Retry failures only is a preview, never a write (plan U5).** A finished signature apply or bulk department run with failures shows "Retry the N that failed" (`_job_failures.html` `retry(job, url)`), which posts `/signatures/retry` or `/users/bulk/retry` (PREVIEWS in the tripwire): the run's own confirm step (`_sig_preview.html` / `_bulk_preview.html` with `retry`) renders in the button's place, `#retry-<job id>`, for exactly the failed people who are still active users (the rest are counted, "left out"), holding them and `job.retry` under a single-use token keyed `retry_key(job id)`, not the live form. Its Apply posts the usual `/signatures/apply` or `/users/bulk/apply` with `retry=<job id>` and no `hx-include` of the page's form — the same `take`, `guard.enforce(..., confirm_step=True)` and typed count — and the new job, titled "… retry — N users", lands in the same slot and can be retried in turn. A job keeps a sample of 200 failures (#9): past it (`job.more`) there is no retry — a retry of the sample would pass for a retry of all — and the panel and the route say so. The stopped department panel lists its failures too (it once showed only "Stopped … Updated N of M").
- **Stop ends a loop between targets, never during one (plan U5).** A mis-scoped bulk apply once had no way to halt. Stop (`POST /jobs/stop`) only sets `job.cancel_requested`; each loop — `_run_apply`, `bulk.set_departments`, `_run_subscribe`, `_run_bulk_onboard` (between hires: one hire's every step runs), `_run_sequence` and `lifecycle.run_offboard` (between steps) — calls `stop_requested(job)` at the top of each iteration, so the write in flight finishes, is recorded and audited, and the run ends with "Stopped by you — N not attempted." Nothing cancels a task mid-`gam` (that is only a quit, `interrupted`). An offboarding marks the rest "– step — not run: stopped" (not `interrupted`) and its Stop asks first (`hx-confirm`, and the route refuses it without `confirmed=1`): stopping between the reset and the revoke leaves the leaver signed in. The calendar index rebuild has no Stop (one call, not a loop). `tests/test_jobs_tray.py` presses Stop during a write on each loop through the strict mock and checks the exact argv that ran.
- **#9, bounded polls.** Every job keeps its bounds in one place, `Job.record`: a `recent` feed of 12 rows, a `failed` sample of 200 with the full count in `failed_total`, 300 chars of reason and of raw error per failure. The final panels print `failed_total` plus "… +K more" past the sample. The three job types once each re-implemented this, with the constants defined twice (plan Q10); these exist because a domain-wide run once made each 1s poll's HTML grow with the user count — and `BatchJob.failed` stayed an unbounded `.append` until 2026-09-23 (failure-log). The Builder sequence sets `window` to its length (at most `MAX_SEQUENCE_STEPS`, 25) so every step keeps its row; offboarding's `log` is its eight steps.
- **Every failure says why (plan U9).** Each loop records the connector's `ChangeResult.remediation` as the reason and its `detail` as GAM's error: the live feeds (signatures, calendar fan-out, Builder sequence, bulk onboarding) show each `✗` row with its reason, and the final panels add GAM's error one click away. The bulk department, fan-out and sequence feeds once listed addresses with no reason.
- **Guarded mutations (invariant #2) — checked in the apply route.** Suspend goes `plan_suspend` → `guard.enforce(previews, form)` → `conn.apply`; the bulk department job, event delete, offboarding, signature apply and onboarding call `guard.enforce` the same way before their first write (see `docs/domains/guard-audit.md` for the list and the exempt single-target LOW writes). The preview route renders the confirm step; the apply route refuses a POST without `confirmed=1` — five routes once ran on a bare POST because only the template asked (failure-log 2026-09-23). A confirm step that posts the page's live form (`hx-include`) also carries its preview's `web/previews.py` token, and the apply route runs the held values: the bulk department job sets the previewed department on the previewed people (their *current* titles, re-read from the directory; someone no longer active refuses the run), and a department, list or group edited after Preview is refused with "preview again" (the stale "Apply to 1 user" once wrote another department to more people, failure-log 2026-09-23). Calendar delete and user delete re-resolve/re-validate server-side and never trust the client-supplied identity (`_resolve_delete_owner`, exact-case `DELETE` / exact-email confirm).
- **No double submits.** Every control that starts a write or a job carries `hx-disabled-elt` (`"this"` on a button, `"find button"` on a `<form>`), so htmx disables it until the response lands — a double-click can't start two offboardings. `test_every_write_button_disables_itself_while_in_flight` checks every template control that posts to a write route. **Trap:** the attribute is inherited, and `find …` resolves from the element making the request — so another htmx control inside such a form (the account-delete Cancel) finds nothing and htmx 1.9 throws on the null, killing that control. Give it its own `hx-disabled-elt="this"`; the same test fails otherwise.
- **Builder validation surfaces as a friendly error.** A share with a calendar role outside the grammar's `<CalendarACLRole>` makes `add_calendar_acl` / `add_calendar_acl_cal` raise `ValueError`; `/users/calendar/add` and `/calendars/share` catch it and render the amber partial with no `gam` call. Don't coerce a bad value in the route (`/calendars/share` once silently turned any unknown role into `reader`, while the other path passed it to GAM — failure-log 2026-09-23). `test_calendar_share_refuses_a_role_outside_the_grammar`.
- **Delegation checks the address before GAM.** GAM reads a delegate as a `<UserList>` — a bare name becomes `name@<domain>`, a comma targets two accounts — so `/users/delegate/add` runs `_check_delegate` first: not an address (`looks_like_email`, which refuses a comma), the owner itself, or an alias (it names the primary) is refused with no `gam` call; an address not in the cached directory, or a suspended one, renders a warning with **Add anyway** (`confirmed=1`) — a warning, not a block, because a minutes-old account isn't in the cache yet. Add/remove results and errors render *inside* `_delegates.html` (`notice`, with GAM's line in the disclosure), so the list and form never vanish behind an error. Remove asks first (`hx-confirm`), like the calendar-access Remove; both stay single-target LOW writes (failure-log 2026-09-23).
- **Cross-tenant safety.** `_index_ready` serves search only when `status.domain == audit_domain`; an index built for another tenant is treated as "needs rebuild" (`test_calendars_search_ignores_other_domains_index`).
- **Directory data into attributes (invariant #8):** templates put Google-sourced strings in autoescaped contexts; never `| tojson` in a double-quoted attribute.

## Gotchas / mock-lies traps
- **Placeholders and example data stay generic** (`example.com`, "Sales", "Training Calendar") — the public repo was de-branded once and the operator's company names crept back in via form placeholders and the seeded onboarding role. The local pre-commit hook's private-term tripwire (`.claude/private-terms`, gitignored) now blocks that. The UI says *Department*, never "store".
- **Record an outcome with `job.record(item, ok, reason, detail)`, never `job.failed.append(x)`** — the append bypasses the cap (a source tripwire fails on it). `job.failed` is a *sample* of `Outcome`s: count with `failed_total`, not `failed | length`, render `job.more` as "+K more" (the `failures` macro does), and compare names with `failed_items`.
- **A 200 proves nothing.** Every route answers 200 whether the write worked or not — a failure renders the amber error partial. Assert success with `tests/helpers.py`: `assert_ok_partial(r)` (no amber box), the `gam_calls` fixture + `gam_writes()` (the exact argv the mock received), and the audit record's `ok`. The mock fails any argv it has no handler for, so an unhandled write can no longer "succeed".
- **Never poll a job's status endpoint in a loop under `TestClient`** (that hung CI for 6h, before the fixtures were context-managed). To assert what a route-started job did, `wait_for_job(client, job)` awaits its task on the client's own loop, then render the status once; the signature apply, bulk store, offboarding and builder-sequence route tests do this. Other job routes (calendar share fan-out, index rebuild, bulk onboarding) still only assert the job started, with completion covered by executor tests.
- Share/subscribe, secondary-calendar delete, event delete, org/vacation writes are only exercised against `tests/fixtures/mock_gam.sh`. Per invariant #3 and CLAUDE.md "the mock lies," a mutation is unproven until run against a throwaway real tenant — check `gamgui/resources/gam7/GamCommands.txt`, not memory, for syntax (e.g. formatjson-reject quirks).
- Bare-address ambiguity: an ACL scope like `sales@example.com` may be a person or a group; `_subscribers_for` resolves it against the cached directory first (a known account is always a person) before a member lookup, because "member lookup errored" is a weak signal — mistaking a person for an empty group silently skips the subscribe that is the whole feature.

## Testing / live-verification status
`.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q tests/test_users_web.py tests/test_groups_board.py tests/test_calendar_index.py`. **Keyboard and focus (plans A2, A4, A5).** The same module's `test_the_keyboard_alone_works_the_tabs_and_each_confirm_panel` sends real key events over CDP (`Input.dispatchKeyEvent`) and reads `document.activeElement`; with a page error trap it fails on any uncaught error too.
- *Tabs.* The user-detail and onboarding strips are ARIA tabs: `role=tablist` > `role=tab` (`id="tab-<name>"`, `aria-controls="panel-<name>"`, `aria-selected`) and `role=tabpanel` (`aria-labelledby`, `tabindex="0"`). `app.js` keeps a roving tabindex — only the selected tab is a Tab stop — and Left/Right (wrapping), Home and End select and show, as a click does (automatic activation). The template renders the first tab selected. User detail's panels are lazy (plan U13): the Overview is served whole from the cached directory, and each other tab's GAM reads (`[data-lazy]`, `hx-trigger="tab-shown"`) load the first time the tab shows — `app.js` fires the event, after `DOMContentLoaded` when the page is still loading, since htmx binds the page then. A strip marked `data-hash` keeps its tab in the URL's `#hash` (`history.replaceState`, no new entry), so a reload, Back or Forward reopens that tab and loads it.
- *Names.* Every `input`/`select`/`textarea` has a wrapping `<label>`, a `<label for>`, `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` — a placeholder or `title` is not a name. Visible labels where the layout has room (vacation subject/message, the welcome email, the role editor, Calendars' event dates); `aria-label` on search boxes and one-line pickers. A Builder slot is a `div[data-slot]` with a `<label for="slot-<key>">` (a wrapping label may not hold the hint buttons), its help line wired by `aria-describedby`; `insertHint` finds the field through `[data-slot]`. A repeated button names its row, visually hidden: a catalog row's Build/Copy carries the command (`<span class="sr-only"> {{ c.name }}</span>`; Copy's visible word is `[data-label]`, so `copyText`'s "Copied" flips only it), a delegate's Remove the address.
- *Focus.* A confirm/preview panel or an error that a click or submit swaps in carries `data-focus` on its heading (`tabindex="-1"`) or primary control (the account delete's typed address); `app.js`'s `htmx:afterSettle` handler focuses it and remembers the control that opened it per zone. A swap from `load`, polling, typing or a changed select never moves focus (the triggering event must be a click or submit). When the panel closes: a Cancel marked `data-cancel` (one that re-renders the zone with `hx-get`) or the `clear` action returns focus to the opener, or to its re-rendered twin (same tag and text — "Suspend…"); a Confirm or Run that replaced the panel leaves focus on the zone itself (`tabindex="-1"`), so its result is read. A panel whose error line is new puts `data-focus` on the error, not the heading. A click or submit whose swap replaced the control it came from, with no panel or opener involved (the title/department Save — `_org_form.html` swaps itself out — a delegate's Remove), used to drop focus to `<body>`: `regainLater` waits for every root of that swap to settle (out-of-band ones settle first) and puts focus on the control's re-rendered twin (same tag and text — the Save, `aria-describedby` its "Saved ✓"), or, when it is gone, on the zone (`#delegates`, opening with "Removed …"). Focus that survived the swap is left alone; `test_a_swap_that_replaces_the_focused_control_puts_focus_back`.
